ATtiny13A / internal RC oscillator 9.6 MHz / CKDIV8 unprogrammed (disabled)
Low fuse: 0x7A
High fuse: leave factory value unchanged (typically 0xFF).

Example (verify chip, wiring and programmer before writing fuses):
  avrdude -c usbasp -p t13 -U lfuse:r:-:h
  avrdude -c usbasp -p t13 -U lfuse:w:0x7A:m
  avrdude -c usbasp -p t13 -U flash:w:synth.hex:i

The firmware also writes CLKPR=0 after the timed unlock sequence.
F_CPU = 9600000, PWM Timer0 Fast PWM TOP=255 (37,500 Hz), PB1/OC0B.
