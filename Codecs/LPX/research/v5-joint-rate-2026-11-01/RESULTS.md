# Frozen five-arm pilot: results

The pilot found one small numerical size/quality frontier: a complete short
non-speech profile 4 record went from 635 to 634 or 633 stored bytes while passing
every current whole-record gate. This is 0.157% or 0.315%, not a 5% reduction.
No speech record became smaller while preserving all gates, and no arm admitted
a whole record at 95% or 90%. These results do not establish audible equivalence,
improved firmware Flash, a default change or a full-corpus effectiveness claim.

## Scope and proof

The predeclared pilot completed exactly 12 contexts, 60 arm records, 100 actual
reset helpers and 180 derived cap decisions. Both profile workers exited 0 and
passed terminal source/input guards. There were no failed, interrupted, repeated
or omitted helpers, no new initial fits and no native builds. The separate
preflight completed 10 synthetic helpers; the workers paid another 10 warm helpers.
All 25 research contracts pass, including the actual saved-input/native-closure
contract. The full 213 production checks were reported passing by integration
before launch; this research did not rerun that suite or change production code.

The Python/model epoch is b21cb028bdb2ae3c285fbc2d9ccac96ca3c447d0 in the explicit
frozen joint-rate worktree. Preparation used the earlier e8632a4e epoch and its
unchanged level policy. The strongest canonical plus positively target-admitted
matched-CPU union supplied 104 baseline contexts, 82 replacements and 14 already
smaller seeds. Only the 12 predeclared full pilot contexts were searched; each
received fresh complete baseline model/common/v5 PCM/PWM and whole metric checks.
The stored whole metrics agreed exactly before price derivation and search.

Host-native libraries retain their original SPI preparation epoch and exact
callback-enabled build recipe. Every linked C/header/table/callback source and
library fingerprint was checked; the current model epoch is bound separately.
The build-JSON indexing limitation is stated in [PREFLIGHT.md](PREFLIGHT.md).
This host bridge is not a new AVR/LTO/player proof.

The saved-decision projector independently reconstructed every exact whole recipe
from actual captured bytes, checked all 100 helper settings/journals, reproduced
all 180 choices and current-baseline gates, and checked exact new-study cache keys
and creating-event provenance. It performed no decoding, metrics or search.
There are 182 stored whole rows: 82 fresh measurements and 160 exact reuse events
across those rows plus 60 before-search baseline checks. All 7430 captured/fallback
model-SSE checks cover 264,661,211 sample evaluations. Whole endpoint validation
verified 113 distinct reset streams / 5,359,862 samples and 452 native domain
comparisons. Intermediate local frontier points do not acquire whole admission.

## Limits and next decision

The private inventory retains 26 distinct changed eligible ordered tuples across
fixed-cap, minimum-preserved-quality and eligible-frontier roles. It has been
handed off for a finite target-cost plan only; this pilot launched no images.
No full-corpus extension is authorized or run. The two long NS records remain
structural diagnostics; they were not searched by this short complete-record
pilot. A future corpus test must let all 60 of their resets change.

The useful next mechanism question is how to reach the reserved spectral and
cross-rate branches fairly within the paid helper budget. That is a new,
separately frozen experiment, not a reason to broaden this unchanged mixed pilot,
weaken quality floors or present tiny numeric changes as audible improvement.
The reused corpus has already been examined, and no independent heldout claim
follows. [diagnostics.json](evidence/diagnostics.json) is explicitly post-pilot;
its descriptive analyses did not influence settings, gates, candidates or calls.
