# Targeted listening observation and playback-level choice

After comparing the new result with the old result, the user described the
**new example as much better** and explicitly chose automatic amplification of
very quiet inputs for the new voice mode. This is a strong informal preference
for this one pair, not a controlled corpus listening score.

The complete 32,000-sample / 8 kHz source and old native PCM16 result received
the same fixed 64x presentation gain. The new result was already encoded from
the source at that declared gain. Every presentation file was independently
read back sample-exact; no clipping, trimming, resampling or separate fitted
gain was used. This was an open comparison, and the source had already become
a user-targeted diagnostic following a perceived change of voice character.
It is not independent held-out evidence for these new choices.

At this common presentation level, the source has 77 candidate-period windows.
The old result has no paired reliable periods; the new result has 59, with 58
within the diagnostic's 100-cent tolerance and a median absolute paired error
of 2.07 cents. The new fixed-PWM result has 57 paired periods, all within that
tolerance, and 2.11 cents median paired error. Missing windows remain failures
or unknowns. These are overlapping estimator windows, not independent samples
or validated true-F0 labels. Listening does not turn those proxies into a
universal preservation guarantee.

The selected new stream occupies 1,702 bytes under the original 1,800-byte
cap. Its declared playback gain is 64. The unchanged-level frontend trial did
not find a new automatically admissible target stream; it retained the old
control. Therefore the new result is not reported as an equal-original-volume
quality gain. [Complete player measurements](../quiet-voice-player-2026-10-28/README.md)
retain that level distinction separately from data, Flash and timing.

Earlier feedback that A/B sounded nearly equal concerns the **two older**
1,800/1,701-byte alternatives. It remains valid for that different comparison.
The present feedback concerns the new 1,702-byte voice-mode result versus the
old result at the common presentation level.

The source, listening WAVs, mapping, private feedback receipt and media/output
fingerprints remain in ignored local storage. User-approved level-policy
activation is separate from the frozen corpus epoch and will follow its
source-bound checks; historical research receipts are not rewritten.
