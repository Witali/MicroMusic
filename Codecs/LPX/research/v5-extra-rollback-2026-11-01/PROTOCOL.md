# Prospective four-stage whole-chain gate and rollback pilot

This is a separate encoder-only experiment, selected after the negative full
existing-extra pilot. Preserve all previous numerical/source epochs. It is not
held out, and a local or full PCM SSE gain is not an audible-quality claim.
Commit the complete harness, metadata policy and finite synthetic preflight
recipe before numerical execution. Production API/source are not frozen yet;
no corpus work starts until integration reviews the final source and preflight.

## Identical inputs and finite scope

Use P4 speech ordinals 20 and 42 only: the exact original pre-joint-rate strongest
baselines, complete prepared targets, original declared levels and reset geometry.
Keep one and five reset streams respectively, including stored unplayed tails.
No later V1, anchor or full-pass winner becomes a seed. Both arms use the same
causal optional-pulse positions, unchanged cheap amplitudes and horizon 160.
Spectra, parameters, mandatory pulses, filters, sample/profile/rate fields,
inactive/unplayed pulses and header bytes [:20] remain fixed. Preserve the extra
flag; only serialized payload-length bytes [20:22] may update consistently.

The fixed real scope is two contexts, two arms, four arm records, twelve stateful
reset-pass instances and twelve final 100/95/90 cap decisions. Each instance
gets one cumulative 49*initial_eligible trial allowance, no CPU cutoff, no group
revisit and no transferred budget. Eligibility is frozen from input: amp != 0
and 40*group+position < actual samples. New extras are not added. Every eligible
group is processed once; zero eligibility is an honest no-work reset. Preserve
zero-amplitude position aliases as semantically inactive, as in the prior pass.

Let B be actual complete input bytes and use local cap min(B,65535). Final whole
caps are B, floor(.95B), floor(.90B), with no per-cap refit. Data caps do not stand
in for full firmware Flash measurements. No initial fitting, parameter search,
DP mixtures, decoder/format/default change, target image or simulation is allowed.

## Four shared coverage stages

For reset i with initial eligible count M_i, stage k targets ceil(k*M_i/4),
k=1..4. Process each reset to that exact cumulative count, in original reset
order, then assemble one complete ordered recording. Repeated/zero goals do
not revisit a group. Evaluate the entire recording, including analysis windows
crossing reset boundaries; no per-reset quality gate substitutes for it.

- continue: keep the uninterrupted working path regardless of stage admission;
  retain each whole attempt. It is a freshly executed control, not an old metric
  replay. Check its actual common-slot bytes against the previous saved causal
  uninterrupted path under an explicit unchanged operator/DSP bridge.
- rollback: before each block retain owned snapshots of the protected working
  fields. Accept the proposed block only if every unchanged full gate passes
  relative to the exact fresh original anchor AND (joint score,actual bytes) is
  lexicographically no worse than the current protected incumbent. Use exact
  score ordering here, as canonical selection does. A 1e-12 tolerance is only
  for descriptive rank ties, never permission to worsen the protected pair.

On rejection restore the block's optional-pulse fields and live active count,
retain cumulative paid trials/visited/covered and the consumed eligible cursor,
then rebuild exact DSP state to that current consumed prefix. Do not reuse
filter/history/ring state from the rejected stream. Take the next protected
snapshot at the rebuilt cursor; never retry the rejected groups or rewind their
budget. Subsequent blocks therefore depend prospectively on this gate decision.

Snapshots are owned by one pass instance and cannot alias live fields, reports
or another reset's state. Record snapshot/rollback calls and recomputed prefix
samples separately. The protected whole stream is input initially, then the
last accepted complete proposal; it is independent of the final per-cap choice.

## Bounded attempts and durable retention

Each arm/context retains baseline plus at most four stage proposals, hence at
most five distinct whole quality-measurement attempts. Across all real arm records
there are sixteen stage decision events and at most twenty distinct whole
attempts, including baselines. Deduplicate only exact ordered bytes under the
identical target/source/runtime context; keep each stage-to-candidate mapping.
No fifth proposal, extra local recipe or product of per-reset choices is added.

Persist every serialized tuple and metadata immediately, before native/metric
work can fail. Label it unverified until complete common/v5/model PCM/PWM and
full-quality checks finish. Persist each completed value/gate decision before
the next stage, rollback or measurement. Retain rejected tuples too. Interrupted
or unverified attempts remain evidence and cannot become automatically admitted
points. Keep the last completed protected state and journal; a failure does not
authorize a rerun or any larger budget.

Both arms make their final unchanged canonical budget selections over all their
completed fixed attempts. A proposal rejected for worsening the protected pair
may still be a valid smaller-cap tradeoff if it passes the original anchor gates;
retain it in the final pool. No new search or relaxed gate occurs at selection.

## Fresh checks and complete comparison

Measure the original full anchor and every distinct attempted whole chain freshly
under the exact frozen execution epoch: common and v5-only native plus independent
model PCM/PWM, complete PCM/PWM SSE/STOI, period coverage, clipping and joint
voice/noise gates. Native binaries may be reused only after reopening their exact
binary/compiler/flag receipts and unchanged C/header/table closure. Bind new
Python model/metric source and runtime separately from the old native epoch.

Use the exact original target/level, unchanged metric/DSP dependencies and recorded
runtime bridge to compare saved V1, anchor and current full-pass admissible whole
pools. Old measurements stay old; never substitute them for a fresh baseline or
new whole measurement. Include all saved eligible points at the same context,
including nonselected and above-B points; deduplicate actual ordered bytes only.

The success test is a new admissible size/joint frontier point beyond the union
of all those prior pools AND every admissible fresh continue-control point.
Check all rollback points, including nonselected ones, and all twelve actual cap
decisions. A lower local loss, more accepted blocks, return to input, or superiority
only to the original seed does not satisfy this test. A successful fresh control
alone is reported separately from any incremental rollback benefit. No default
adoption or playback quality claim follows without later evidence.

## Costs, validation and privacy

Bind the exact frozen root/module bytes and origin, prepared manifest path plus
digest, native closure, package/thread runtime and complete preflight receipts
before each real worker. Require closed synthetic-only summary/attempt, identical
research/production/native/runtime context and the identical prepared manifest.
No source changes or automatic restarts while numerical jobs are live.

Pure contracts cover exact lex/gate acceptance, common ceil goals, repeated slots,
bounded deduplication and keeping every attempted tuple. Source contracts must
prove old-wrapper byte/field/trial parity, owned snapshots, exact rollback output
and active count, retained progress/budget, future-prefix parity and partial tails.
The fixed synthetic recipe is three complete contexts, with both real policies:

1. The published 640-sample P4 causal-cancellation fixture: one initially active
   played extra at group 5, position 30, amplitude -8; the target changes only
   that extra to position 0. Preserve the original spectra, parameters and base
   pulses from the deterministic causal probe.
2. A two-reset, 1280-sample concatenation: the same first input/target plus a
   second unchanged copy of the original 640-sample input as its exact target.
   Keep both resets and measure the complete recording across their boundary.
3. The 201-sample prefix recipe with extra 5 still at position 30, now unplayed,
   and a deterministic alternating +/-2 PCM target perturbation. It has zero
   eligible groups and trials; preserve its complete stored packet and tail.

Preflight is six synthetic arm records/eight pass-instance constructions. Each
real arm worker warms the same three contexts/four instances. Thus the new study
has twelve synthetic arm records/sixteen synthetic instances, separate from its
twelve real instances; count 24 preflight, 24 warm and 16 real stage events even
when exact-byte deduplication reuses a measured point. Natural gates remain
unchanged; no forced outcome is injected into numerical preflight. Root's direct
state/native rollback contracts and pure injected controller rejection/retention
contracts cover rejection wiring if these synthetic real gates accept. Any prior
study's synthetic costs remain prior evidence, not new calls in this campaign.

An open per-session process-time budget spans interleaved other sessions and whole
