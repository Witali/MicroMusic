# Frozen proposal: exact allocation over retained V1 pools

This is a separate saved-pool experiment. Preparation may reopen metadata and
hash immutable inputs after this recipe is committed. DP on actual saved pools,
native preflight and fresh whole metrics require integration review first.
No result or execution permission is implied by this document alone.

The fixed input is the completed five-arm V1 pilot: 60 arm records, 12 exact
source/profile/declared-level contexts and 100 searched reset pools, from both
original profile workers. Its 100 paid helpers remain prior work. This study
performs zero encoding, fitting, refinement, excitation adaptation or new native
builds. The live anchor-order pilot and its pools are excluded.

V1 producer/model epoch is `b21cb028bdb2ae3c285fbc2d9ccac96ca3c447d0`.
The new producer is `654ada493202f565d24cb9dcf22f16137c825329`. Bind its exact
committed production sources. Reopen the closed V1 export, terminal workers,
source/runtime/input graph and exact serialized stream bytes. Bridge every old
production file unchanged except the explicitly identified host trajectory
ordering source; new source files are bound separately. Reopen the original
callback-enabled common/v5 native receipts, binaries, compiler and full C/header/
table closure against the new epoch. A renamed binary or matching filename is
not sufficient. No old metric cache becomes a fresh measurement.

For each reset, the DP pool is exactly its retained V1 frontier (at most 16
points) plus the original baseline, selected and raw rows required by the old
recipes. Reproduce the V1 frontier rule before restriction. Deduplicate actual
bytes with stable old IDs and require exact verified integer local PCM SSE.
Do not widen to other raw captures, current anchor-study outputs or another arm.
Preserve every original ordered baseline/control recipe independently of DP
dominance, even if oversized or worse in additive SSE. Keep all resets, actual
stored tails and the original prepared target level.

Call the reviewed exact allocator once per arm at its original whole B, with
250000 working states and 10000000 transitions. Rate is the actual sum of stored
byte lengths, including each header/tail/padding. Distortion is Python-integer
additive local PCM SSE. Derive minimum-SSE fitting endpoints at
`floor(B * percent / 100)` for 100/95/90 from this same complete frontier. There
is no per-cap DP, helper call, lambda grid, retries or resource-bound increase.
Exactness concerns only the supplied finite pools and this additive criterion.
STOI, period/F0, PWM/voice/noise and joint quality are not claimed additive.

During DP callbacks, retain the best completed full tuple independently for each
of the three fixed caps. On an explicit state/transition limit, the arm remains
incomplete and exact=False; do not claim global minima or pool no-fit. Freshly
gate at most the three retained completed cap winners with explicit nonexact
labels. Partial prefixes are never whole candidates. Preserve callback winners
in private failure evidence if an exception interrupts execution; do not silently
restart a failed attempt.

The final whole pool is the unchanged original five role recipes plus at most
three DP tuples, deduplicated by exact ordered bytes. Freshly verify baseline,
controls and DP endpoints through the model and common/v5 native PCM/PWM. Use
one new-study exact cache bound to target, level, source/native context and
ordered streams; retain its creating event and every reuse. Old whole metrics
are agreement checks only. Reuse the closed V1 integer pool-SSE audit with its
explicit provenance, and freshly compare additive SSE against reconstructed
whole endpoints. Track raw-SSE reuse, fresh integer checks, model/native samples
and fresh/cache whole measurements separately.

Apply unchanged full PCM/PWM SSE/STOI, clipping, period coverage and joint
voice/noise admission against the original same-level baseline. Rank with the
existing whole `choose_budget`; lower raw SSE alone is not an admitted win.
Compare the old-five-only pool with the full union at each cap. Primary success
requires a new admitted tuple absent from all five originals and either a lower
joint score, smaller bytes without worse joint score (tolerance 1e-12), or a new
admission where the originals have none. Resource-limited wins remain separate
descriptive retained-candidate results, excluded from exact primary successes.
Report all ties, failures, raw-only/no-fit decisions and original controls.

The declared primary remains within each arm. A saved-only secondary compares
each arm/cap choice with the UNION of all five V1 arms' original whole recipes
for the same exact context. Those originals are already freshly checked in this
60-record campaign. Deduplicate exact ordered bytes and reuse their fresh values;
perform no additional DP, native check or metric call and do not seed any search
from this union. Separately identify a point already known in another V1 arm.
A genuine context-wide size/joint frontier extension must be absent from that
original union and not dominated by an admissible original with no larger bytes
and no worse joint score (tolerance 1e-12). Report view counts and distinct
context/cap/tuple counts, separating exact from nonexact retained candidates.
Include all already-measured added DP tuples in this frontier projection, even
when the within-arm minimum-joint-score policy selects a different larger point.
Keep selected-choice comparisons as a separate table; this changes no primary.
Improving a weaker within-arm control alone is not overall codec progress.

Single-reset cases are identity controls against direct minimum-SSE selection
from their same restricted pool at each cap. An interior lower-cap frontier point
can be absent from the five extremes even with one reset: classify that as cap
selection, never cross-reset mixing. Do not require the entire single-reset union
to equal the old five. Multi-reset gains remain a distinct mechanism category.

Before real DP, pass source-stable synthetic contracts and a separate native
preflight with fixed tiny two-reset fixtures in both profiles. Preflight uses
no corpus streams or helper fits and is not an effectiveness outcome. Keep
preparation, preflight, DP, baseline/whole metrics, native/model verification,
persistence and full workflow CPU/wall costs separate. Equal data pools do not
make new allocation or admission work free. Do not sum overlapping clocks.

All private paths, inputs, streams, PCM/media/ELF fingerprints, native receipts
and binding graphs stay under ignored build storage. Public evidence is limited
to anonymous ordinals, numeric outcomes and source/toolchain provenance. No
target build, playback, firmware-Flash, physical-board or audible-quality claim
is authorized by this study. New joined tuples need separate target admission.
