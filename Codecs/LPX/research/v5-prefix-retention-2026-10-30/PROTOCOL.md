the completed V2 audit exposed deeper-search nonmonotonicity. Its only material
is anonymous speech source ordinal 42, profiles 4 and 6: the two multi-reset
rows in the paired default-budget subset. Both contain five actual resets.
It is a targeted correctness diagnostic, not held-out or corpus-general evidence.

The frozen active implementation is `5a6cd1ab`. Do not edit that core, V1,
the original V2 protocol/driver/receipts, or the fixed 75-chain player union.
No encoding, seed fitting, refinement, operator evaluation or per-cap refitting

## Finite prefix pool

For every saved local search, depth 0 exposes candidate IDs whose initial
origin is `control` or `seed`. Each completed stage then exposes its `outputs`.
Retain visible IDs and rank them by frozen static `(score, file_bytes, id)`:
admissible-and-fitting best and merely fitting raw best. Static admission must
agree with the audited fixed-anchor PCM/PWM SSE/STOI, clipping, period-coverage
and joint-objective floors. A local search exhausted before depth 4 retains
its last visible pool. Missing local output makes that complete choice absent.

Construct complete ordered chains at the **same** depth 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 for
all five resets, with selected and raw views at each depth. No independently
varied per-reset depths, Cartesian mixtures, beam search or global allocation
search is allowed. The original default's three measured whole choices are
mandatory. Also retain every actual screen whole choice selected or named as
raw tradeoff at the original 100/95/90 caps as a mandatory fallback. Deduplicate
by the exact tuple of ordered reset bytes, preserving every provenance label;
concatenated bytes alone are insufficient to identify reset behavior.

## Whole-chain evaluation and selection

Every distinct chain is compared with the exact source target and original
default whole rollback using the same complete PCM16/PWM metrics, source-bound
voice/noise diagnostics, clipping and joint-objective floors. Count every
header, stored tail, optional marker and byte of every reset in the whole cap.

Previously audited whole metrics may be reused only when exact ordered bytes,
reset geometry, full target bytes, speech/level policy, objective descriptions,
core/source/runtime and auditor provenance all match. Recheck input hashes and
record/marker identities; identify the exact cached record and chain privately.
Report reuse separately from new measurement. Every new unique whole chain
must be fully decoded with the independent model and both common/v5-only native
PCM16/PWM receivers, then measured over the complete source. Local row metrics
are never added or substituted for full-chain metrics.

Apply these same finite chains to integer caps `floor(B*percent/100)` at
100/95/90. Rank eligible chains by joint score, actual bytes, stable discovery
order. An admitted incumbent remains available. At each cap the selected
result must be no worse in `(score, bytes)` than every already known feasible,
admitted screen/default selection under the same full gates. Keep raw/no-fit
rows distinct from automatic admission. This is retained finite history, not
a proof of globally optimal encoding or minimum bitrate.

Journal each replay attempt before measurement, retain unsuccessful attempts,
pin sources/inputs before and after, and record fresh/reused whole metrics,
native/model work, zero new fit/refinement calls and runtime scope honestly.
All media, identifiers, paths and fingerprints remain in ignored `build/`.
