# Matched-search trial: finite complete-player gate

This separate stage measures hardware feasibility and full firmware cost of the
fixed single-path LPC versus trajectory trial. It performs no fitting, helper
search, endpoint selection experiment, decoder change or subjective adoption.
No image may start before both final study shards and the final exporter pass,
all source/input/attempt receipts reopen exactly, and the finite scope is
reported. Numeric mean quality or a nominal helper CPU budget alone does not
adopt trajectory or any larger production representation.

## Actual firmware and playback

Use the unchanged strict dispatcher and `PlayerEvidence` proof from the prior
75-chain union. Its original 118-record source gate remains pinned, while the
new preparation additionally binds complete 248-arm receipts and sources.
Strict reuse indexes include the original 75-chain/150-row campaign and the
retention 8-chain/16-row campaign; only positive exact source/recipe/data/runtime
proof is reusable. Byte length, labels and prior PASS text alone permit no reuse.

Target ATmega328P/AVR5, 16 MHz/8 kHz, explicit ASM, queue 256/prefill 192 and IRQ
loads 0/+100. Both loads of each chain stay in one deterministic parity shard.
Keep the frozen consumer, source files, old artifacts and proof receipts read
only; write isolated attempts/images only under a fresh ignored campaign.

Charge actual complete linked Flash including embedded data, metadata, tables
and program; report program excluding streams and static SRAM separately.
Data alone over 32,768 B is an honest no-fit without a forced build or playback
claim. Smaller data gets an exact full build; distinguish linked overflow from
other build errors. Unavailable complete-image sizes stay unavailable.

For fit images, verify full-chain model/common/v5 PCM16 and PWM, physical PWM
with interrupts active, produced/played samples, EOF, queue, timer ticks/events,
errors, stack and zero underruns. Strict artifact reopening at aggregation and
anonymous export is mandatory. Preserve partial attempts and inspect actual
process handles before any explicit recovery; never retry on a poll timeout.
No C-backend, physical-board, analog-quality or audibility claim follows.
