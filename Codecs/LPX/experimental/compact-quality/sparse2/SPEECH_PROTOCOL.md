## Bind the strongest saved predecessor

The original November2 campaign is locally available:27 declared targets,
54 initial streams and162 follow-up best pointers. Resolve ONLY the27 strict2k
version8/mode15 **round3 final** winners, one per target. Earlier-round pointers
are history, not additional independent cases. The private corpus need not
match a public WAV to be usable for this local comparison.

Before a run, verify every authoritative immutable best pointer, candidate
file/metadata digest, admissible status, target/reference preparation, actual
sample count and sample rate. Use the saved integer target and declared level
policy exactly. Do not infer gain, resample, trim, align, normalize or reconstruct
a target from playback. Recheck baseline model/native PCM and PWM through both
unchanged reference receivers. A mismatch is a binding failure, not a weaker
baseline to substitute silently.

The binding audit found that original raw-source absolute paths are stale, while
all27 saved prepared integer targets exist. Their pinned, verified bytes are
the authoritative optimization references for this comparison. Use them
unchanged with the saved declared policy; recovering a raw WAV is not required
when target hashes and predecessor binding pass. Record that original source
preprocessing was not independently rerun. If a prepared-target hash fails,
stop that dependent case instead of reconstructing or substituting it.

Keep source and stream hashes, identifying paths and full metadata in ignored
private receipts. Retained public reports use anonymous case ordinals and the
original frozen group membership only. No private audio, targets, stream/model
payloads, identities, paths or fingerprints are committed or uploaded. Reusing
these local data does not authorize publishing them.

The separately verified307-byte public Chego fixture is a valid preserve-policy
example but is not asserted to be the campaign's final predecessor. It is not
the confirming baseline for this study. No weaker fresh initial encode or
public-only substitute is used merely because it is easier to reproduce.

## Frozen operator and admission

Use the unchanged [mechanism protocol](MECHANISM_PROTOCOL.md), implementation
and independent native receiver build hashes that passed the synthetic tests.
The deterministic witness establishes only a pruning-loss mechanism, not a
speech gain. Freeze the speech harness and its source hashes before the pilot.

At most4 complete frames, at most3 frozen legal symbols per innovation, all
at most81 leaves per frame, unchanged full suffix and exact real prefix state.
The global exhaustive shortlist is12 distinct changed complete leaves by PCM
SSE; greedy/beam2/beam4 controls retain their own at most4 complete frame winners.
Report/admit each control independently against the same saved baseline. Do not
mix their wins into exhaustive results, chain frame patches or add another pass.

Admission reuses the existing `refine_sparse80_pulses` complete-output equations:
PCM/PWM SSE and available STOI non-regression, both source-frozen voice floors,
clipped counts, the fixed PWM band-error totals, burst maxima and every partial
tail-window floor. Require a changed actual PWM output and strict improvements
of both the old mean joint and PWM joint objectives. All native/model samples,
non-pulse bits, original byte length, stored padding and EOF must agree. Each
case retains its exact saved baseline outside search and selection.

Count every symbol screen, leaf, control extension, synthesis sample, native
verification and complete metric evaluation. Duplicate bytes can share measured
metrics but remain explicit attempts. Record actual wall/CPU time and separately
identify one-time compilation/warm-up and independent-validation work. No source
or criterion tuning from speech outcomes is permitted.

## Run and reporting

Pilot: the first original tuning-speech case in the frozen manifest's stable
order. Use it only to verify infrastructure/measurement; do not tune afterward.
Then process all27 final2k cases exactly once with the same frozen implementation,
including the pilot case if it is reused as an immutable verified result. The
pilot is not an extra independent sample.

Preserve the original20 speech /7 negative-control classification and the
original tuning/heldout split, including the heldout ten speech targets and
their fixed quiet/noisy variants. STOI is not a music-quality score; do not drop
failed or missing scores to improve an aggregate. Keep unchanged cases in all
denominators. Report speech/group outcomes and each finite control separately.

Report saved baseline, best generated raw exhaustive proposal and admitted final
output: actual bytes/rates, PCM/PWM error sums and STOI, old objective, pitch/voice,
clipping and frequency/tail guards. For heldout speech use the original fixed
membership, mean STOI and summed-energy SER; do not average decibels. Report
small numerical gains as small. A proxy improvement is not a listening preference
or a percentage increase in perceived quality.

Retain all private artifacts and authoritative per-case journals locally before
advancing a result pointer. Publish only code, anonymous numerical evidence,
software provenance and honest limitations. Decoder arithmetic, parser, tables,
format, declared level and actual bytes stay fixed. A host/native PASS is not
complete linked AVR interrupt/underrun, stack/Flash or physical-board evidence;
make no new hardware-support claim without those separate measurements.
