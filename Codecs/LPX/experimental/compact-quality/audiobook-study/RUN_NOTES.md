# Execution notes

The first pilot dispatch was denied by the WSL sandbox before the native
executable started. Independent inspection found the operating-system denial,
empty standard error, no generated native artifacts and no encoder outcome.
The failed attempt and its original freeze remain preserved privately.

A separate recovery directory links that evidence to a new orchestration
freeze. Its scientific sources, prepared targets, memberships, compiler options
and executable are identical. Only the permission used to dispatch WSL changes.
Independent verification of both freezes passed before the elevated dispatch.
This infrastructure recovery did not repeat an observed encoding or search.

The registered development pilot represents one continuous 160,000-sample
case. Remaining development cases were released after its independent outcome
audit; held-out cases stayed locked until the explicit final-policy freeze.

The recovered pilot passed all nine artifact checks and complete native PCM,
native PWM, model and EOF replay. Its optional raw candidate was smaller and
improved waveform error and STOI, but the unchanged production pitch guard
rejected it. The selected output is the exact finished baseline. Raw numerical
improvement is therefore a proposal result, not an admitted quality improvement.

The completed study contains twenty successful scientific cases, each encoded
once with one baseline call and one optional helper call. The denied dispatch
produced no extra scientific outcome. Development completed 12/12 cases with
three selected changes and four optional safety rollbacks. After the explicit
final-policy freeze, held-out testing completed 8/8 cases with no selected
changes: six optional safety rollbacks and two unchanged proposals. Optional
rollbacks retain the exact successful baseline and are counted separately from
whole-case failures. Both phase validations passed all 180 saved artifacts,
with full continuous native PCM/PWM, model, byte-accounting and EOF replay.
The held-out result does not confirm a quality benefit from the extension.
