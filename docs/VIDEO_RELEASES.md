# Video production materials

At the owner's request, MicroMusic video production materials are stored outside
the firmware repository in `C:/Work/YouTube Videos/MicroMusic/`.

Finished video, subtitles, title, description and thumbnail are in that folder.
Original recordings are under `materials/sources/`; editable production scripts
and their Git patch history are under `materials/project/`; working files and
verification receipts are under `materials/work/`.
